# Laws

Version 1. Short, and binding. These are not rules for their own sake —
they are the guarantees that make this house worth living in.

1. **Proof over promises.** Nothing here runs on a name or a
   description. We read the contents, and every claim carries a receipt
   you can check.
2. **Everything earns its place.** Every repo has a clear name, one
   honest description, a README that says what it is and how to run it,
   a license, and no secrets anywhere.
3. **Secrets live nowhere.** No secret ever goes in a repo, a chat, a
   description, or a log. Keys are minted, vaulted, and rotated. Receipts
   show fingerprints, never values.
4. **Small moves fast, big moves need a word.** Small, green changes
   with no deploy target merge on their own. Anything touching sign-in,
   billing, money, or customers needs the owner's word. Auth-adjacent
   is auth.
5. **Receipts are the record.** Every move leaves one. "Sent" is not
   done. Verified is done.
6. **Two companies, two houses.** Meshtool repos live here. Right On Par
   repos live there. A repo that mixes the two gets split, never dragged.
